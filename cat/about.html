About UNTIL WE MEET AGAIN

Veterinary Hospice & Euthanasia Services

At Until We Meet Again, we understand that saying goodbye to a beloved pet is one of life’s hardest moments. Our compassionate team is dedicated to providing personalized care through quality-of-life evaluations, comfort-focused hospice support, general exams, and vaccinations. When needed, we offer gentle, individualized euthanasia services to guide families through difficult end-of-life decisions. Our mission is to create a peaceful, respectful environment where pets feel safe, loved, and dignified in their final moments—because every companion deserves tender care until the very end.

Team Members

Get to Know Our Amazing Team Members!

Dr. Tejpaul Bhatia

Owner, Francis Petcare Hospital, Richmond, BC Founder & Previous Owner of Cypress St. Animal Hospital, Vancouver

Meet Dr. Bhatia, a Veterinarian with a passion for delivering exceptional care, Dr. Bhatia brings over 40 years of experience in Veterinary Medicine. His areas of expertise are small animal, internal medicine, Dermatology, emergency medicine, and critical care.

A graduate of the College of Veterinary Medicine in PAU India (1986). He immigrated to Canada in 1997. With his expertise across different veterinary clinics in Alberta & Vancouver his unparalleled compassion for animals strived him to open Cypress St. Animal Hospital in 2002. While success and so many awards were thriving his way for more than 22 years, suddenly his health challenged him to take a break and retire from Cypress but his commitment and dedication brought him back in action and he started his practice once again in Richmond. Dr. Bhatia prioritizes continued education, completing 50-70 hours of professional development annually. Outside of work, he loves watching sports like Cricket, Field hockey, Ice hockey and also is a devoted follower of spiritual & religious themes.

Reshma Gajavelli, BVSc

Veterinary Technician

Reshma Gajavelli, BVSc, is a compassionate Veterinary Technician with a strong clinical foundation, earning her degree from PV Narasimha Rao Veterinary University in Hyderabad, India. She moved to Canada to continue her education and advance her career in veterinary medicine.

Reshma is known for her gentle handling, attention to detail, and client-focused care. Her interests include small-animal medicine, anesthesia, dentistry, preventive care, radiology, and client education. She values lifelong learning and is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate veterinary care.
