Pet Memorials

Fond Memories offers a wide range of meaningful memorial options to help families honor their pets with love and dignity. Their collection includes unique non- standard urns, remembrance jewelry, and other thoughtfully crafted keepsakes. They also provide a peaceful cemetery with private burial options, along with online memorials where families can celebrate and share their pet’s story. While cremation viewing is temporarily unavailable due to ongoing renovations, all other services continue to be offered with the same compassion, care, and respect.
